About

I identify the right problem, then solve it through agentic interface design: interfaces that don't just respond, but act.

I'm a product designer working on agentic AI interfaces: systems that act on behalf of people, not just respond to them. Right now I design AI agents in production at Pinnacle AI, automating store-manager work for a 10-location coffee-shop chain through conversation.

I got here in steps. I started as a design engineer, spending about 70% of my time in code, and ended that job design-led. I studied human-computer interaction at Indiana University Indianapolis, founded Aithraa, spent a fellowship at Perplexity AI, and now build agents that a real business runs on.

I start with the work itself rather than the request, and I'd rather say plainly why something stopped than dress it up. Two of the ventures here were shut down or paused; the case studies say why.
